# Privacy policy for Další díl

Last updated: 28 September 2026

Další díl has one purpose: to show the next episode of series a user has rated on ČSFD.

## Data the extension handles

The extension reads the signed-in account indicator, profile rating pages, and episode pages on `www.csfd.cz` and `www.csfd.sk`. From those pages it derives the user's series progress and next-episode links.

Další díl does not collect passwords, authentication cookies, payment information, or data from other websites. It never changes ratings or other ČSFD data.

## Storage and sharing

Derived series progress, the selected ČSFD profile, display count, language, and cached links are stored only in Chrome's local extension storage on the user's device. This data is not sent to the developer or any third party. The extension has no analytics, advertising, remote server, or separate account.

Requests to ČSFD use the user's existing browser session and HTTPS. Data is used only to provide the extension's visible next-episode list.

## Retention and deletion

Local data remains until it is replaced by a later scan, cleared with Chrome's extension data controls, or removed when the extension is uninstalled.

## Changes and questions

Material changes to this policy will be published in this repository before an updated extension is released. Questions and privacy requests can be filed through the repository's public issue tracker.
